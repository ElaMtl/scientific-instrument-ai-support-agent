\*Current State Observation

Most customer support knowledge is not stored in a centralized repository. It exists primarily as tacit knowledge held by the company founder and product inventor. As a result, customer support operations are highly dependent on a single individual, creating a significant scalability and continuity risk.

- BUSINESS RULES

All inquiries are received through a shared support email inbox.
All inquiries are handled by a single individual acting as Founder & Product Expert.

Inquiry categories may include:

- Product Information
- Pricing Request
- Quotation Request
- Shipping Inquiry
- Technical Support
- Software Support
- Scientific Support
- General Inquiry
- Most responses are prepared manually.
- Standardized response templates are limited.

* KNOWLEDGE SOURCES

1. Primary Source

- Founder & Product Expert's personal expertise and memory

2. Secondary Sources

- Company website
- Product documentation
- Knowledge Base
- Previous email conversations
- Google Sheets
- Internal files
